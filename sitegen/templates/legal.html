What this site does not do

This site sets no cookies, runs no JavaScript, loads no external resource (no fonts, no images, no scripts, no stylesheets from third parties), and contains no analytics, no tracker, no advertising and no affiliate link. There is no account, no form and no field to fill in. Nothing you do here is measured, stored or transmitted anywhere.

This is enforced by the build, not merely promised: every page is checked before publication for the absence of any third-party sub-resource.

Because the site is served by GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical connection data (including your IP address) in order to deliver the pages, under its own privacy statement. That processing is GitHub's, not the publisher's, and happens for any site hosted there.

Personal data in the content

The site publishes player names or handles, tournament names, dates and placements, alongside decklists expressed as card identifiers and quantities. Player names are personal data.

  • Source. These results were already published by the tournament organisers listed under Sources. Nothing here is collected from you, and nothing is obtained from a private or restricted space.
  • Purpose. To let players import competitive decklists into the OPTCGSim simulator, and to compare lists within an archetype. Nothing else.
  • Legal basis. Legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)): documenting published competitive results, with data already public, in a form limited to what that purpose needs.
  • Minimisation. No email address, no postal address, no photograph, no ranking history, no cross-tournament profile beyond what a decklist page shows.
  • Retention. For as long as the corpus keeps the tournament. There is no separate database of people.

Removing your name

If you appear here and would rather not, say so and it will be removed — you do not have to justify the request. Open an issue on the repository naming the tournament and placement. You need not disclose anything further in public; naming the entry is enough to identify it.

Removal is applied to the published corpus and takes effect on the next build, normally within twenty-four hours. You also have the rights of access, rectification, objection and restriction under the GDPR, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in France, the CNIL).
